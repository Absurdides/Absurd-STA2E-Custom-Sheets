STRESS TRACK ⓘ
STRESS & RECOVERY // 2E RULES
Max Stress: Equal to your Fitness attribute (species, talents and other factors may change it).
Avoid Injury: When you suffer an Injury, suffer Stress equal to the attack's severity to ignore it.
Breather: A few minutes of rest removes 4 Stress.
Break: At least half an hour, up to a couple of hours, removes 8 Stress.
Sleep: Several hours of rest removes all Stress, up to your normal maximum.
Recover Stress (2 Mom, Rep): After a successful task, remove 1 Stress from yourself or an ally who can hear you (max 3 per task).

INJURIES ⓘ
INJURIES & MEDICAL TREATMENT // 2E RULES
Defeated: When you suffer an Injury you are Defeated: you fall prone and cannot act for the rest of the scene.
Avoid Injury: Suffer Stress equal to the attack's severity to ignore the Injury (and not be Defeated by it).
Protection: Reduces the severity of an Injury you suffer by your Protection, to a minimum of 1.
Stun Injury: Lasts only while you are Defeated; removed at the end of your next turn after you stop being Defeated.
Deadly Injury: While Defeated with a Deadly Injury you are Dying, and die at the end of the scene without medical attention.
First Aid: Daring + Medicine, Difficulty 2, within Reach: the patient is no longer Defeated. Or, Difficulty = the Injury's severity to treat an Injury (it then imposes no penalty).
Long-term Healing: Control + Medicine, Difficulty X and X hours (X = severity). Add 1 to Difficulty and complication range if the Injury was not treated.

WEAPON QUALITIES // 2E COMBAT RULES
Accurate: With the Aim minor action, re-roll up to two d20s instead of one.
Area: On a hit, spend 1 Momentum (Repeatable) per extra target in the same zone. May Succeed at Cost.
Charge: With the Prepare minor action, add Area, Intense or Piercing to the attack (Area reduces severity by 1).
Cumbersome: Cannot attack unless you take the Prepare minor action the same turn.
Debilitating: Difficulty to treat or heal its injuries is +1.
Grenade: Throwable up to Medium range; enough for three attacks.
Hidden X: Minor action to conceal; searching needs Insight or Reason + Security, Difficulty X.
Inaccurate: You do not benefit from the Aim minor action.
Intense: Increase severity for 1 Momentum instead of 2 (Repeatable).
Piercing: A successful attack ignores the target's Protection.

STARFLEET OPERATIONS MANUAL // STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES // 2E CORE
CORE TASK RESOLUTION & ACTION ECONOMY
TASK RESOLUTION // D20 RULES
Target Number (TN): Attribute + Department. Roll 2d20 (up to 5d20 with bonus dice); each d20 equal to or under the TN is a success.
Success: Successes equal to or above the Difficulty (0–5) pass. Each extra success becomes 1 Momentum.
Critical Success: With an applicable Focus, a d20 rolling ≤ Department scores 2 successes. With no Focus, a roll of 1 scores 2.
Complications: A d20 rolling 20 (or in the complication range, max 5) causes a Complication. Buy it off by adding 2 Threat.
Assisting: One assistant is free; each extra costs 1 Momentum or 1 Threat. Each rolls 1d20 with their own TN and Focus. Counts only if the leader scores 1+ success.
ACTION ECONOMY // TURN SEQUENCE
Turn: 1 major action and 1 minor action. No character takes more than one turn per round.
Minor Actions: No task. Aim (re-roll 1d20 on an Attack), Draw Item, Prepare, move up to Medium range. Extra minor action: 1 Momentum.
Major Actions: Normally a task: Assist, Attack, Direct, First Aid, Pass, Sprint. A second major action costs 2 Momentum (task Difficulty +1); max two per round.
Attack: Melee: Daring + Security, Difficulty 1. Ranged: Control + Security, Difficulty 2. Becomes opposed if the target is aware (melee) or in Cover (ranged).
Initiative: Play passes to the opposing side after each turn. Spend 2 Momentum to Keep the Initiative.
MOMENTUM SPENDS // 2E CORE CATALOG
GENERAL TASK SPENDS
Create Opportunity (1–3 M): Before rolling, buy up to 3 extra d20s (1st: 1, 2nd: 2, 3rd: 3 Momentum). Max 5d20.
Create a Trait (2 M): Create, change, increase/decrease potency of, or remove a trait.
Obtain Information (1 M): Ask the GM 1 question about the scene; must be answered truthfully.
Reduce Time (2 M): Halve the time a task takes (not in action scenes).
COMBAT & ATTACK SPENDS
Added Severity (2 M): +1 attack Severity per 2 Momentum (max +2).
Counterattack (2 M, Imm): Opposed Attack won by target: inflict an Injury on the attacker.
Disarm (1–2 M): Target drops weapon in Reach (1 M one-handed, 2 M two-handed).
Recover Stress (2 M): Remove 1 Stress from self or ally (max 3 per task).
TACTICAL & REACTION SPENDS
Swift Action (2 M): Take 1 additional major action (task Difficulty +1).
Extra Minor Action (1 M): Take 1 additional minor action (each once per turn).
Keep the Initiative (2 M, Imm): Hand turn to an ally; not again until opposition has acted.
Difficult Terrain (1–3 M, Imm): Pay to leave difficult terrain or cross an obstacle.
DETERMINATION [MAX 3]
STRESS, INJURY & RECOVERY
Perfect Opportunity: Before rolling (after buying extra dice), set one d20 to a 1 (critical success).
Moment of Inspiration: After rolling, re-roll any number of d20s in your dice pool.
Special Technique: Gain one talent you do not possess for the rest of the scene. Prerequisites apply; the GM may veto it.
Gaining & Limits: Start each mission with 1; maximum 3. Spend only when a Value or Directive applies. Gain it when your Values conflict with your goals.
Stress: Maximum = Fitness. At maximum you are Fatigued: pick one attribute (tasks using it fail, other tasks +1 Difficulty).
Avoid Injury: When you suffer an Injury, suffer Stress equal to its severity to ignore it.
Breather / Break / Sleep: Breather (a few minutes) removes 4 Stress. Break (30 min or more) removes 8. Sleep removes all.
First Aid: Daring + Medicine, Difficulty 2: patient is no longer Defeated. Difficulty = severity treats an Injury.
Additional Progress (2 M): During an extended task, add 1 to your Impact for 2 Momentum.
