FOCUSES & CRITICAL SUCCESSES // 2E RULES
Focus Rule: When an applicable Focus applies to a task, any d20 rolling equal to or below your Department score scores two successes (a Critical Success).
Assists: If an assisting ally has an applicable Focus, their assist die also scores two successes if it rolls ≤ their Department rating.
Opposed Tasks: Critical successes break ties in opposed task contests.

STRESS TRACK ⓘ
STRESS & RECOVERY // 2E RULES
Stress Depletion: Reaching 0 Stress or taking an attack with Severity ≥ remaining Stress causes an Injury.
Breather (Short Rest, ~5 min): At the end of an action scene, you automatically recover 2 Stress.
Break (Full Rest, ~1 hour): Rest in a secure, non-hostile environment completely clears all Stress.
First Aid: Spending Momentum on a First Aid / Medicine task clears 1 Stress per Momentum spent.

INJURIES ⓘ
INJURIES & MEDICAL TREATMENT // 2E RULES
Avoid Injury: Suffer Stress equal to incoming attack Severity to avoid suffering an Injury.
Stun vs Deadly: Stun clears at scene end. An untreated Deadly injury kills the character at the end of the scene.
First Aid (Stabilize): Daring + Medicine, Difficulty 2 (within Reach). Removes Defeated status and prevents death. Spend 2 Momentum to rejoin the fight immediately.
Treat Injury: Control + Medicine, Difficulty = Injury Severity. Removes trait/complication penalties for the rest of the mission.
Revival: Daring + Medicine, Difficulty 3 (costs 1 Determination). Attempted before the subsequent scene ends to revive a deceased ally.

WEAPON QUALITIES // 2E COMBAT RULES
Accurate: May re-roll 1d20 on ranged attacks at Medium range or further.
Area: Strikes the target and all characters/objects in the target zone.
Charge: Spend a Minor Action preparing the weapon to add +1 Severity.
Cumbersome: Two-handed; cannot fire on the move without penalty.
Debilitating: Target suffers a lingering physical Hindrance trait.
Intense: Target must pay +1 extra Stress to avoid an Injury.
Knockdown: Target is knocked Prone on a successful hit.
Piercing X: Ignores X points of the target's personal Protection.
Stun / Non-Lethal: Inflicts Stun injuries; clears at scene end.
Vicious X: Adds +X Severity / Challenge Dice when an Injury is inflicted.

REQUISITIONS & LOGISTICS // 2E RULES
Standard Issue (Cost 0): Routine gear (Combadge, Uniform, sidearm) carries zero Opportunity Cost.
Mission Allowance: The GM sets an Opportunity Cost budget based on mission profile and department roles.
Field Requisitions: Acquire non-standard gear mid-mission by spending Momentum or adding to Threat.

MOMENTUM SPENDS, COMBAT
1+ MOMENTUM: BONUS DAMAGE
ADD +1 CHALLENGE DIE (Δ) TO THE ATTACK'S FLUID DAMAGE ROLL PER MOMENTUM SPENT. THIS OPTION MAY BE REPEATED MULTIPLE TIMES ON A SINGLE ATTACK ACCORDING TO GENERATED MOMENTUM.
1 MOMENTUM: EXTRA MINOR TASK
PERFORM ONE ADDITIONAL MINOR TASK (SUCH AS AIMING, MOVING, OR DRAWING EQUIPMENT) DURING YOUR ACTIVATION PHASE. A CHARACTER IS STRICTLY LIMITED TO A MAXIMUM OF ONE EXTRA MINOR TASK PER ENCOUNTER TURN.
1 MOMENTUM: OBTAIN INFORMATION
ASK THE GAMEMASTER ONE DIRECT QUESTION ABOUT THE SCENARIO OR ENVIRONMENT. THE GM MUST PROVIDE A TRUTHFUL ANSWER BASED ON ACCESSIBLE DATA AND WHAT THE CHARACTER COULD REASONABLY LOGICALLY DEDUCE.
2 MOMENTUM: KEEP INITIATIVE
PASS OPERATIONAL ACTIVATION DIRECTLY TO ANOTHER ELIGIBLE ALLY INSTEAD OF GIVING UP THE TURN TO OPPOSING FORCES. CANNOT BE SPENT IF ENEMY INITIATIVE IS MANDATED BY AN ACTIVE COMPLICATION OR ENCOUNTER TRAIT.
2 MOMENTUM: CREATE ADVANTAGE
ESTABLISH A BRAND-NEW TACTICAL SCENE TRAIT OR ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITION ON THE BATTLEFIELD THAT ADVANTAGEOUSLY ASSISTS ENCOUNTER ALLIES OR CREATES A DIRECT PERFORMANCE PENALTY FOR OPPOSING HOSTILE FORCES.
2 MOMENTUM: DISARM
A SUCCESSFUL ATTACK WITHIN REACH OR CLOSE RANGE KNOCKS ONE WEAPON OR MANUALLY ACCESSED ITEM HELD BY THE TARGET AWAY TO THE GROUND, FORCEFULLY REQUIRING A MINOR TASK TO RETRIEVE. NARRATIVE SENSE APPLIES.
