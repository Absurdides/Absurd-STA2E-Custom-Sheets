MOMENTUM SPENDS, COMBAT
1+ MOMENTUM: BONUS DAMAGE
ADD +1 CHALLENGE DIE (Δ) TO THE ATTACK'S FLUID DAMAGE ROLL PER MOMENTUM SPENT. THIS OPTION MAY BE REPEATED MULTIPLE TIMES ON A SINGLE ATTACK ACCORDING TO GENERATED MOMENTUM.
1 MOMENTUM: EXTRA MINOR TASK
PERFORM ONE ADDITIONAL MINOR TASK (SUCH AS AIMING, MOVING, OR DRAWING EQUIPMENT) DURING YOUR ACTIVATION PHASE. A CHARACTER IS STRICTLY LIMITED TO A MAXIMUM OF ONE EXTRA MINOR TASK PER ENCOUNTER TURN.
1 MOMENTUM: OBTAIN INFORMATION
ASK THE GAMEMASTER ONE DIRECT QUESTION ABOUT THE SCENARIO OR ENVIRONMENT. THE GM MUST PROVIDE A TRUTHFUL ANSWER BASED ON ACCESSIBLE DATA AND WHAT THE CHARACTER COULD REASONABLY LOGICALLY DEDUCE.
2 MOMENTUM: KEEP INITIATIVE
PASS OPERATIONAL ACTIVATION DIRECTLY TO ANOTHER ELIGIBLE ALLY INSTEAD OF GIVING UP THE TURN TO OPPOSING FORCES. CANNOT BE SPENT IF ENEMY INITIATIVE IS MANDATED BY AN ACTIVE COMPLICATION OR ENCOUNTER TRAIT.
2 MOMENTUM: CREATE ADVANTAGE
ESTABLISH A BRAND-NEW TACTICAL SCENE TRAIT OR ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITION ON THE BATTLEFIELD THAT ADVANTAGEOUSLY ASSISTS ENCOUNTER ALLIES OR CREATES A DIRECT PERFORMANCE PENALTY FOR OPPOSING HOSTILE FORCES.
2 MOMENTUM: DISARM
A SUCCESSFUL ATTACK WITHIN REACH OR CLOSE RANGE KNOCKS ONE WEAPON OR MANUALLY ACCESSED ITEM HELD BY THE TARGET AWAY TO THE GROUND, FORCEFULLY REQUIRING A MINOR TASK TO RETRIEVE. NARRATIVE SENSE APPLIES.
